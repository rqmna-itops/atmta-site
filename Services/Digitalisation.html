Digital Passport

Digital Passport — a smart solution that enables rapid, on-demand production while removing the need for physical spare-part storage. Each component is given a secure digital identity containing all data required for reproduction via CNC or additive manufacturing. The process begins with 3D scanning and modeling, followed by linking material and manufacturing data. A unique Digital Passport, tagged with aQR or RFID code , is then created and stored for future use. When needed, the part can be instantly reproduced from this digital record. With OIG’s Digital Passport Service, companies gain immediate access to production-ready part data, achieving faster repairs, reduced downtime, and smarter, more sustainable operations.

Digital Passport

Details

Content

  • 3D CAD model, and engineering drawing .
  • Part information sheet.
  • Manufacturing route (CNC, additive, or other).
  • Material specification.

How It Works

  • Scan and model the part in Siemens NX.
  • Link material and manufacturing data.
  • Create a QR/RFID-tagged Digital Passport.
  • Reproduce on demand via CNC or 3D printing.

Key Benefits

  • On-demand part production with full traceability.
  • Lower inventory costs.
  • Sustainable digital storage.
  • Seamless CAD/PLM/ERP integration.

Applications

  • Spare parts for industrial machinery.
  • Legacy and discontinued components.
  • Critical systems in oil & gas, energy, and transport sectors.
  • On-demand manufacturing environments.
